# Do schools kill creativity? — summary

Sir Ken Robinson's TED talk runs twenty minutes and was published by TED in 2007 [M].

- Children starting school that year will retire around 2065, and nobody can say what the world will look like even five years ahead [V1 02:16].
- Kids will take a chance when they do not know the answer, because they are not frightened of being wrong [V1 05:35].
- If you are not prepared to be wrong, you will never come up with anything original [V1 05:45].
- National education systems now treat mistakes as the worst thing you can make [V1 06:06].
- No education system on the planet teaches dance every day the way it teaches mathematics [V1 09:06; V1 09:15].

Gillian Lynne was sent to a doctor as a child, who told her mother she was not sick but a dancer [V1 16:54].
